Delete your account

Smidd · Norsk versjon

You can delete your account and everything that belongs to it at any time. You do not have to give a reason.

How to do it in the app

Open Smidd and go to Settings → Delete account. You will see exactly what gets deleted, and you have to confirm once before anything happens.

How to do it without the app

If you no longer have the app installed, send an email to the address below from the same email address the account is registered to. Write "Delete my account" in the subject line.

kagfrom@hotmail.com

We reply to confirm that the request has been received, and again once the deletion has been carried out.

What gets deleted

All of it. Specifically:

  • Your account and login credentials
  • Every meal, food item and nutrition entry you have logged
  • Weight, body measurements, calorie budget and all history
  • Workouts, exercises, sets, personal records and progress
  • Daily readiness, sleep, energy and any cycle data
  • GPS tracks from runs
  • Conversations with the AI coach

What is not deleted immediately

Backups. The server is backed up routinely. Your data may remain in a backup for up to 30 days after deletion, and disappears when that copy is rotated out. It is not used for anything in the meantime.

Technical logs. Server logs without health data may contain timestamps and error messages for a short period, for operational and security reasons. They are rotated continuously.

The shopping list. Items you have added to the shopping list are not deleted automatically along with the account. They contain no health data — only item names — and you can delete them yourself in the shopping list at any time.

How long it takes

If you delete from within the app, it happens immediately. If you send an email, we process the request within 30 days, which is the deadline under the GDPR. In practice it is faster.

Changed your mind?

Deletion cannot be reversed. If you are unsure, you can sign out instead and leave the account standing — it costs you nothing.
