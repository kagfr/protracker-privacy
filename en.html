Privacy Policy

Smidd · last updated 23 August 2026 · Norsk versjon

In short. Smidd collects health data because the app cannot work without it. We never sell it, show no ads, and use no analytics or cross-app tracking.

At any time, you can download everything we hold about you, or delete all of it for good — directly in the app.

2. What we process

Account

We store your email address, date of birth where the app version used collects it (older accounts and older app versions: year of birth only), password as a cryptographic hash (never the password itself), health-data consent with timestamp, and the account creation time. No name, no phone number, no street address.

Health and training data

  • Nutrition: meals, foods, calories, protein, carbohydrates, fat, micronutrients and water intake.
  • Body and goals: weight, weight history, height, age, gender, activity level, calorie budget and goals.
  • Daily readiness: sleep, energy, mood and stress.
  • Training: strength sessions with exercises, sets, weight, repetitions and perceived exertion; cardio and interval sessions with duration.
  • Cycle data: start date and duration of menstruation, calculated cycle phase, and symptoms you choose to log. See section 5.
  • Medications: medications you add yourself, with doses and the times you took them. They are stored on your phone only and are never synced to our server — not even if you have an account.
  • Mental wellbeing: mood and daily check-ins you log yourself.
  • Progress photos: photos you take of yourself. They are stored on your phone only and are never uploaded to our server.
  • From Health Connect: sleep, steps and resting heart rate. The app only reads — it never writes anything back to Health Connect.

Location

GPS tracks from runs you start yourself. Location is also used if you search for gyms nearby — your position is then sent to Google Maps to perform the search. It is not stored by us, and is only sent when you actively search. See sections 4 and 7.

Microphone and camera

  • Voice logging: speech recognition happens on your phone. If the text is hard to interpret, the finished text — not the audio — is sent to the AI provider. Audio recordings never leave the device and are not stored.
  • Barcode scanning: the camera reads the barcode locally. The image is not stored and is not sent anywhere.
  • Text recognition (InBody printouts): read locally on the device. The image is not stored.

Shopping list

Items you add. The shopping list is personal and is not shared with other users. Your health data is never shared with anyone. Note that shopping list items are not deleted automatically when you delete your account — they contain no health data, and you delete them yourself in the shopping list. See the deletion page.

What we don't do

Smidd uses no analytics tools, no cross-app tracking and no advertising SDKs. There is no Firebase, Crashlytics, Sentry or anything similar in the app. We sell no data, never share health data with advertisers, data brokers or insurance companies, and build no marketing profile of you.

If the app crashes, the error message is written to a file on your phone and shown to you next time you open the app. It is never sent anywhere automatically — you decide whether to share it with us. The report contains the error message, app version and phone model, not your health data.

Home screen widgets

If you choose to add a Smidd widget to your home screen, it can show health figures (such as calories) outside the app — including when the phone is not unlocked by you. Using widgets is entirely your choice.

3. Legal basis

Health data is a special category of personal data under GDPR Article 9. We process it only with your explicit consent, cf. Article 9(2)(a). You give this consent at onboarding and can withdraw it at any time.

  • Delivering the app's features: Article 6(1)(b), combined with explicit consent for health data.
  • AI features (coach, meal photo, voice interpretation): data is sent only at the moment you use the feature. No background sharing — if you don't use them, nothing is sent.
  • Location: separate consent, requested the first time you start a run.
  • Microphone and camera: separate consent, requested at first use.
  • Operations and security: legitimate interest, Article 6(1)(f).

The consents are independent. You can use Smidd without the coach, without GPS, without the microphone and without the camera.

4. About GPS tracks

GPS tracks are stored on your phone only. They are not synced to our server and never leave the device. If you uninstall the app, the tracks are gone.

Location is among the most revealing data there is — it shows where you live and when you are away. That is why we chose not to let it leave your phone at all.

5. About cycle data

We record the start date and duration of menstruation, calculate your cycle phase, and store the symptoms you choose to log. The cycle section appears automatically when you have set your gender to female in your profile, and does not exist in the app otherwise.

Cycle data is health data under Article 9 and is processed under the same strict requirements as everything else. It is used only to show you your own cycle and to put training and readiness in context. It is never shared, and you can delete it separately.

8. Sharing with a personal trainer (PT)

You can choose to give a named PT you invite yourself access to selected data. This is how it works:

  • Everything is off by default. No one sees anything until you actively turn it on.
  • You choose per data type — sessions, weight, nutrition, runs, readiness — each one separately.
  • The PT can never request access. No such feature exists. The initiative is always yours.
  • You can revoke access at any time, without giving a reason. The PT is not notified.
  • Access ends automatically if you leave the gym, switch gyms, or the PT stops working there.
  • Coach conversations and meal photos can never be shared — not even if you choose "share everything".

9. Gyms

If you join a gym with a gym code, you get access to the gym's programmes and branding in the app. The code never gives the gym access to your data.

The gym never sees individuals. The gym only sees aggregated figures — for example how many members trained in the past week — and only when the group is at least 10 people. No names, no individual data, no list of who has not trained. What a PT sees through sharing (section 8) is never included in the gym's figures.

If you leave, you lose the gym's content — but keep all of your own data.

10. Purchases

In-app purchases (training plans) are made through Google Play. We never see your card details. What we receive is a purchase token from Google, which our server uses to verify that the purchase is genuine. Once you have bought a plan, you keep it.

11. What the server logs

Server logs contain the method, path, status code and duration of each call. Nothing more.

Request and response bodies, login tokens, passwords, email addresses and voice text are explicitly redacted before anything is written to a log. This is enforced in code, not just a principle.

The logs contain no health data, are rotated continuously, and are used only for debugging and abuse prevention.

12. How long we keep data

  • Your account and data: until you delete them. We never remove anything on our own.
  • After deletion: gone from the database immediately. May remain in a backup for up to 30 days before it is rotated out, and is not used for anything in the meantime.
  • Login tokens: 30 days, after which you must log in again.
  • Server logs: see section 11.

13. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction, data portability and to object to processing. Two of these are built directly into the app:

Download everything we hold about you. You get a complete file with your account and all logged data, in a machine-readable format. This is data portability under Article 20.

Delete everything. Settings → Delete account. You confirm with your password, and the account, all health data and all sessions are gone in one operation. This cannot be undone. See also the deletion page, which works without the app installed.

You can withdraw any consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of what was already done. You can complain to the Norwegian Data Protection Authority (Datatilsynet) if you believe we handle your data incorrectly.

14. Security

  • All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are stored as hashes, never in clear text.
  • Login tokens are stored encrypted on your phone.
  • Login, registration and lookups are rate-limited to prevent abuse.
  • API keys live only on the server and are never part of the app.
  • In the event of a personal data breach, Datatilsynet is notified within 72 hours, and you are notified directly if the breach poses a high risk to you.

15. Age limit

Smidd is for people aged 18 or over. Health data is a special category, and under the Norwegian DPA's guidance the age of consent for processing such data is 18.

16. Changes

If we change this policy in a way that matters to you, we will tell you in the app before the change takes effect. The date at the top shows when it was last updated.
